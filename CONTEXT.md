# Slaps

A macOS menu bar app that puts notices on screen you can't miss: meetings, reminders, and whatever other apps send.

## Language

**Alert**:
A notice due at a moment, shown in your face (full screen, a banner or a card at the side), one at a time, and gone
once answered.
_Avoid_: Notification, popup

**Slap**:
An Alert shown full screen, whatever the style setting says.
_Avoid_: Takeover

**Source**:
Who sent an Alert or Tab (CI, Boiler Room, a script), as the sender names itself; a Tab's colour comes from its
Source unless the sender picks one.
_Avoid_: App, origin, sender name

**Tab**:
A lasting, low-key notice pinned to the edge of the screen; many can be up at once, and each stays until dismissed.
_Avoid_: Sticky, note, toast, badge

**Escalate**:
A Tab past its Deadline leaves the edge and becomes a Slap; snoozing that Slap puts it back as a Tab.
_Avoid_: Promote, upgrade

**Deadline**:
When a Tab escalates: set by its sender, or by the default in Settings, which is none. A Tab without one never
escalates.
_Avoid_: Timeout, expiry, TTL
