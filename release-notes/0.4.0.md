## A card at the side

A fourth alert style, beside full screen and the banners: a card in the top right of your main screen, under the menu
bar, like a notification that stays until you deal with it. It leaves your keyboard and the rest of the screen alone.
Choose it in Settings › General › Style.

## Alerts you can answer

Other apps can now put buttons on their alerts, and Slaps opens that app's link with the one you press. Boiler Room
uses this: when one of your scripts asks "Rotate the key?", you can say Yes or No from the alert itself.

For apps and scripts that send alerts:

- `actions=Yes,No` with a `callback` URL puts answer buttons on an alert. An alert can keep its `link` beside them.
- `id=` names an alert: one sent again with the same id takes the old one's place, and `slaps://dismiss?id=` takes
  back just that one.
- `style=` gives one alert its own style: `fullscreen`, `banner`, `top`, `bottom` or `side`.
- Links and buttons open web pages, or apps Slaps knows answer alerts (Boiler Room for now).

## A command line

`slaps alert "Deploy done" --in 5`, `slaps dismiss --id q3`, `slaps pause --minutes 30`, and `slaps help` for the rest.
Settings › General › Command line puts `slaps` in `~/.local/bin`.
