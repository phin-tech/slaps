## Tabs

Notices that don't take over the screen: small colourful tabs on the edge of your main screen, like Growl's, that stay
until you dismiss them. Point at one and it opens into a card with its title, notes, link and buttons. If a tab has a
deadline and you leave it past that, it becomes a full-screen slap; snooze the slap and it goes back to the edge.

Scripts, CI and bots send them, and they wait for you instead of interrupting:

- `slaps tab "Review #412" --from GitHub --deadline 60`, or `slaps://tab?title=…`, or `show tab` from AppleScript.
- `deadline=` is minutes, a time, or `never`. Without one, Settings › Tabs › "Unless the sender says, slap me" decides;
  by default they never slap.
- `group=` stacks a sender's tabs into one pile with a count, like a heap of review requests. `icon=` puts an SF Symbol
  beside the count.
- `position=top`, `middle` or `bottom` lets a sender keep its tabs in their own place on the edge.
- Each sender gets its own colour; `color=` picks another. `id=` replaces a tab, and `slaps://dismiss?id=` takes it back.

In Settings › Tabs, choose the edge and where on it, how they look (an icon, slim or wide), regular or mini, neat or
messy piles, and whether they show in screenshots. They hide while you share your screen, and the menu bar lists every
tab, with Dismiss All.

## Your own shortcuts

Every shortcut can now be changed in Settings › General, including the new one for tabs (⌃⌥⌘T), which opens them to
work from the keyboard: arrows to move, Return to open, Delete to dismiss.

## Settings when you open it again

Opening Slaps while it is already running, from Finder or Spotlight, now opens Settings.
