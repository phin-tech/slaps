## Move tabs around

Drag a tab, or a pile of them, up or down the edge and it stays where you drop it. Drop it on other tabs to join them
in one place; anywhere else, it stands on its own, and tabs that end up close together make room for each other.

Where you put a tab wins over where its sender asked, and it stays there when the sender sends it again; a pile's new
tabs join it where you left it. To send one back to its sender's place, open it and choose **Put Back Where It Was
Sent** from its ⋯ menu.
